44.	What is the difference between let and const?
        let can be reassigned and cannot be redeclared it is a block scope
        const cannot be reassigned or redeclared and it is a block scope
45.	What is the difference between var and const?
        var is global scope and functional scope, can be reassigned and redeclared 
        const is block scope, cannot be reassigned and redeclared
